Senior Backend Engineer — Remote (United States)

We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to design and operate APIs used by millions of
customers. You will lead service design, improve reliability, and mentor engineers across the
team. This is a remote role for candidates located in the United States.

Required qualifications
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience.
- Strong production experience with Python.
- Hands-on experience designing REST APIs with FastAPI or a comparable Python framework.
- Production PostgreSQL experience, including schema design and query optimization.
- Experience deploying containerized services with Docker and Kubernetes.

Preferred qualifications
- Experience on AWS and with infrastructure as code.
- Kafka or another event-streaming platform.
- A bachelor's degree in computer science or equivalent practical experience.

You will collaborate with product and security partners, participate in an on-call rotation, and
help the team improve its engineering standards. We value clear communication, pragmatic design,
and candidates who can explain tradeoffs rather than simply list technologies.
